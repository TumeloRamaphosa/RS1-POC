### 3.2 Deployment Methodology

**Phase 1: Site Assessment + Design (Weeks 1-4)**
- TL Engineering conducts site survey (EthSwitch HQ, 21st floor Zemen Bank Building)
- ART Engineering finalizes modular DC specs (power, cooling, security)
- Alibaba Cloud designs private cloud architecture (VM sizing, storage, network)
- Studex maps Agent OS integration points (EthSwitch APIs, monitoring tools)

**Phase 2: Infrastructure Delivery (Weeks 5-12)**
- ART MODULAR unit shipped from Moscow to Addis Ababa (8 weeks transit)
- TL Engineering prepares site (foundation, power feed, fiber connectivity)
- ART installs modular DC (2 weeks assembly + testing)
- Alibaba Cloud deploys Apsara Stack (private cloud software)

**Phase 3: AI Platform Deployment (Weeks 13-20)**
- Alibaba Cloud installs AI services (fraud detection, credit risk, AML)
- Studex deploys Agent OS (Control Hub port 42069, 5 agents)
- Integration testing: AI platform ↔ EthSwitch payment switch (ATM, POS, QR, IPS)
- User training: EthSwitch IT team, business users

**Phase 4: Data Analytics + Go-Live (Weeks 21-26)**
- Alibaba DataWorks configures ETL pipelines (transaction data ingestion)
- QuickBI builds executive dashboards (real-time transaction monitoring)
- Studex Agent OS generates morning/evening briefs (7am/9:30pm EAT)
- **Go-Live:** Production cutover, 90-day warranty period begins

## 5. Compliance & Risk Mitigation

### 5.1 Data Sovereignty & Security

**Compliance Commitments:**
- **Data Residency:** All EthSwitch data stored in Addis Ababa (ART DC), no cross-border transfer without NBE approval
- **NBE Regulations:** Compliant with National Bank of Ethiopia IT infrastructure guidelines for financial institutions
- **PCI DSS Level 1:** Alibaba Cloud payment gateway security (cardholder data protection)
- **ISO 27001:** ART DC information security management (certified)

**Security Measures:**
- **Physical Security:** NtechLab facial recognition (access control), 24/7 CCTV monitoring
- **Network Security:** Anti-DDoS protection, WAF (web application firewall), VPC isolation
- **Data Encryption:** AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit
- **Access Control:** Role-based access (RBAC), multi-factor authentication (MFA)

### 5.2 Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| **Import delays (ART DC)** | Medium | High | Pre-clear customs, use DHL air freight for critical components |
| **Forex challenges (USD payments)** | High | Medium | 60% ETB billing via local partners, USD only for imported hardware |
| **Power instability (Addis Ababa grid)** | Medium | High | ART DC includes N+1 UPS + generator backup (72-hour runtime) |
| **Skills gap (EthSwitch team)** | Medium | Medium | Comprehensive training program, 24/7 remote support |
| **Integration complexity** | Low | High | Studex project management, phased rollout, 90-day warranty |
